# FAQ

**Can I export everything?**
Yes. Workspace admins can export all notes as Markdown and attachments as a zip
from Settings > Export. Large workspaces can take a few minutes.

**What happens when I downgrade?**
Existing notes stay readable, but you cannot create new ones above the Free limit
until you remove some notes or upgrade again.

**Do you have a mobile app?**
Yes, for iOS and Android. It works offline and syncs when you reconnect.
