# Pricing

Corvid has three plans.

- Free covers 100 notes and 1 GB of attachments.
- Team is $8 per seat per month and adds shared spaces, comments, and version history.
- Business is $20 per seat per month and adds single sign-on, audit logs, and a
  99.9% uptime SLA.

Audit logs and single sign-on are Business-only. They are not included in the Free
or Team plans.
